The problem

Booking a flight home shouldn't feel like a gamble

Today's OTAs were built for leisure tourists with Western passports. For Australia's international students and recent migrants, the same booking flow hides costs, risks, and dead ends.

01

Visa anxiety

A cheaper fare through a transit city can mean a visa you don't have. No major OTA warns you before you book — you find out at the gate.

02

Opaque fees

The headline price balloons at checkout with booking fees, card surcharges, and FX margin. You commit before you ever see the real total.

03

Thin Asia inventory

Incumbents optimise for AU domestic and Europe. Asia-Pacific carriers, transit combinations, and home-city routes are an afterthought.

04

No student deals

No .edu verification, no pay-later, no understanding of term-break seasonality. Students fly 1–3× a year and get treated like everyone else.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What does "visa-aware search" actually mean?
You enter your passport once. Before any fare is shown, Routebird screens it for two things: whether you're eligible to enter the destination, and whether you'd need a transit visa for any layover city. Fares you can't legally fly are filtered or clearly flagged with the transit-visa requirement — so the cheapest option you see is always one you can actually board.
Which passports do you support at launch?
Launch focus is the corridors our community flies most: Indian, Chinese, Vietnamese, Filipino, and Nepalese passports on Australia → India / China / South-East Asia routes. We're adding more passport profiles and corridors each release.
Is Routebird a real booking platform or just a search engine?
A real OTA. We issue live flight tickets via Duffel (merchant of record, so we don't need an IATA number to start) and attach hotels, insurance, and eSIM through affiliate supply. You book and pay on Routebird — not redirected off to someone else's checkout.
When are you launching?
We're in early access now. The waitlist gets the first invites to the MVP — visa-aware flights plus hotel attach on the AU → Asia corridors — ahead of public launch. Join below and we'll be in touch.
How are prices "all-in"?
Every fare shown includes taxes, airline fees, and our card-surcharge estimate — the number you see is what you pay. Routebird earns a transparent convenience fee and affiliate commission, not hidden markups. This aligns with Australian Consumer Law expectations on misleading pricing.
Is Routebird a licensed travel agent?
Australia has had no mandatory travel-agent licence since 2014. ATAS accreditation is voluntary — Routebird plans to pursue it for added credibility as we scale. We operate under the Australian Consumer Law and register for GST once turnover exceeds A$75k. See the footer for the full picture.
